    What Kp measures

    Kp is a unitless planetary index on a scale from 0 to 9. Each value covers three hours. DONKI timestamps the end of each interval. A ground disturbance or high Kp alone cannot establish whether someone saw an aurora.

    Source records and data use

    Natural Resources Canada operates the Victoria and St. John’s magnetic observatories. INTERMAGNET maintains observatory data standards and distributes these definitive records through the British Geological Survey. Aurora Trace averages and baseline-corrects the X component.
